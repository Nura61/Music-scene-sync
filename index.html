Music-scene

Welcome to the Music-scene project!

This project is designed to help you explore and enjoy music in various ways.

Feel free to browse through the features and functionalities we offer.

Features

  • Discover new music
  • Create and share playlists
  • Follow your favorite artists
  • Listen to curated playlists
  • Explore music genres
  • Connect with other music lovers

Getting Started

To get started, you can:

  1. Sign up for an account
  2. Browse the music library
  3. Create your first playlist
  4. Follow artists you love
  5. Join the community discussions
